## Decisions that remain with the business

1. F3: connector verification records, or an explicit rule for what a VERIFIED record means for a connector.
2. F4: accept that a duplicate refusal confirms an open enquiry.
3. Application and lender progress labels: `docs/CONNECTOR_PHASE1_CONTRACT.md` section B. Until approved, partners see only the lead-level steps.
4. Which document types partners may send (`document_type.partner_upload`).
5. Whether staff with `lead.read` may see a referral's customer name on the connector workspace (today they do, and the mobile number is hidden).
6. Dispute evidence attachments: not built. Existing uploads are tied to checklist items, and a dispute has no checklist. A new subject type is needed first.

## Not verified here

- No browser test was run. The new screens are verified by component tests that render them, not by looking at them on a phone or desktop.
- The OpenAPI document (`openapi/openapi.yaml`) was regenerated on 2026-11-02 and now lists the new routes: connector summary, connector referrals, connector activity, the partner referral detail, the partner document list, the partner upload intent, the partner upload completion, and the portal disputes list. The regeneration also picked up the earlier changes (`partnerUpload` on document types, the required `category` on portal disputes) and run-date default values in two schemas. The isolation inventory checks the `/v1` operations (4 of 4 pass). The `/portal/v1` operations are not covered by the isolation sweep.
- The hosted scanner configuration (`CLAMAV_ADDRESS`) has not been checked. Partner uploads stay disabled on hosted until an authorised operator confirms it.
